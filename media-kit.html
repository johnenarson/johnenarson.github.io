Book description

Nearly every prophet, priest, and king in the Bible ascended the Temple Mount, yet no guide truly tells the story of Jesus’ and his disciples’ deep connection to this holy hill. He called it his “Father’s House,” and zeal for it consumed him. Rich in maps and archaeology, this Guide traces where he walked, taught, and wept. In person or from home, you’ll never see Jerusalem the same.

Short bio

John Enarson is an Evangelical educator, author, and creative director in Israel. For over a decade he has worked with Christians on Temple Mount issues, and he spent five years researching and writing Jesus and the Temple Mount: An Illustrated Guide for Followers of Jesus.

Full bio

John Enarson is an Evangelical educator, author, and creative director whose work combines theology with design. Born in Sweden, he has lived in Israel for 30 years, where he resides with his wife and five children. He is a member of The Society for Post-Supersessionist Theology and serves as the Christian Relations Director at Cry For Zion. As a public speaker and theology student, he works to make complex biblical topics and history accessible to a wider audience. For over a decade he has worked with Christians on Temple Mount issues, including helping produce the first major film on the subject, I Am Israel: The Mountain of the King (David Kiern, 2025). Five of those years were spent researching and writing Jesus and the Temple Mount: An Illustrated Guide for Followers of Jesus.
