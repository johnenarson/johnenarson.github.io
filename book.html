Why this book

Jesus loved the Temple Mount.

Nearly every prophet, priest, and king in the Bible ascended here, and nowhere else in Israel can you stand in so many places where biblical heroes truly stood, and where Jesus himself walked, taught, and prayed.

He called it his “Father’s House,” and his disciples said zeal for it consumed him. From infancy to ascension, his life was bound to this place, yet few of his modern followers identify with his passion. This richly illustrated guide asks why, and aims to make that passion contagious.

Inside the book

Fifteen chapters, site by site.

It leads you through the locations most significant to Jesus and his early disciples, then traces the Mount’s momentous history to the biblical hope still to come.

03
