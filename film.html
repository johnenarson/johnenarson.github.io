Behind the film

Working with David Kiern.

It was a privilege to work with director David Kiern and Cry For Zion to bring I AM ISRAEL: The Mountain of the King to life, the first major film about the Temple Mount.

The film and the book grew from the same conviction: that followers of Jesus belong in the story of this mountain. CBN News featured both together.
