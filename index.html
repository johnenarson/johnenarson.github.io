About John

Educator, author, and creative director in Israel.

Born in Sweden, John has lived in Israel for 30 years with his wife and five children. He serves as Christian Relations Director at Cry For Zion and works to make complex biblical topics and history accessible to a wider audience.

For over a decade he has worked with Christians on Temple Mount issues, including helping produce the first major film on the subject, I Am Israel: The Mountain of the King.
